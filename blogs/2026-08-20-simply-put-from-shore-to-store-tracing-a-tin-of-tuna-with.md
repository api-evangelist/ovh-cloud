That "dolphin-friendly, responsibly sourced" label on your tuna tin is a promise — but how do you know it's true? This piece unpacks "trustless" blockchain systems and shows how supply chain traceability lets you scan a QR code and follow a fish's journey from boat to shelf, using WWF's real-world Pacific tuna tracking pilot as the example. It also lays out blockchain's honest limit: it can prove a record hasn't been altered, but not that it was true to begin with — so trust still starts at the point of entry.
